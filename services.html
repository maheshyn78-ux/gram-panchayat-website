Our Services

We provide essential services to empower our rural community and ensure sustainable development.

Certificate Services

Issuance of birth certificates, death certificates, marriage certificates, caste certificates, and land records.

Property Tax Collection

Manage property tax payments, assessments, and related documentation for residential and commercial properties.

Water Supply & Sanitation

Maintenance of water supply systems, sanitation facilities, and sewerage management for the community.

Road Maintenance

Construction, repair, and maintenance of village roads, pathways, and infrastructure development.

Agricultural Support

Support for farmers including seed distribution, irrigation facilities, and agricultural guidance programs.

Social Welfare Schemes

Implementation of government welfare schemes, pension programs, and support for vulnerable sections of society.

Education & Health

Support for educational institutions, health centers, and programs promoting education and healthcare access.

Public Distribution System

Management of ration distribution, fair price shops, and essential commodities supply to eligible beneficiaries.

Complaint Registration

Online platform for registering complaints, grievances, and feedback regarding public services and infrastructure.
